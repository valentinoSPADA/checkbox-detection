This image is a section of a US residential appraisal form (Fannie Mae 1004 family).

Report EVERY checkbox you can see, using the report_checkboxes tool.

A checkbox is a small square outline, roughly 15-60 pixels per side, that a person can mark.
It is usually adjacent to a short label such as "Urban", "Yes", "No", "Fee Simple".

Report a checkbox whether it is EMPTY or MARKED. Empty checkboxes matter as much as marked
ones -- do not skip them.

Set is_checked to true when the box contains any mark at all: an X, a check mark, a dot, a
filled block, a scribble, or a stroke that overflows the border. Set it to false only when the
interior is genuinely blank.

Do NOT report:
- table cells or data-entry boxes wide enough to hold a word or a number;
- the enclosed spaces inside printed letters and digits such as o, a, e, D, B, 8, 0;
- underlines, ruled lines, or the borders of a section.

Give bounding boxes in pixels of THIS image, measured to the outer edge of the box border,
with x1 < x2 and y1 < y2. Be precise about position; approximate boxes are worse than none.
Set confidence below 0.5 when you are unsure whether something is a checkbox at all.
