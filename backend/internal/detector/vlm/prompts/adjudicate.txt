Each image below is a small region cropped from a US residential appraisal form. A checkbox
detector was UNCERTAIN about each one.

IN EVERY IMAGE, EXACTLY ONE BOX IS RINGED IN RED. Judge only that one. The red ring is drawn
just outside its edge and is not part of the form. Other checkboxes appear in the crop --
directly above, below or beside the ringed one -- and marks inside THOSE are irrelevant. A
ringed box whose own interior is blank is "unchecked" even when the box next to it carries a
large X.

For the ringed box, decide which of these it is:
  "checked"        -- it carries a mark of its own: X, check mark, dot, fill, scribble
  "unchecked"      -- its interior is genuinely blank
  "not_a_checkbox" -- it is something else: a table cell, a letter or digit, a line junction,
                      a fragment of text, or empty paper

A checkbox is a small square a person can mark. A cell containing a number, a word, or an
abbreviation is NOT a checkbox, however square it looks.
