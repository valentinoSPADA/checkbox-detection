Each image below is a small region cropped from a US residential appraisal form. A checkbox
detector was UNCERTAIN about each one.

For every region, decide which of these it is:
  "checked"        -- a checkbox carrying any mark (X, check mark, dot, fill, scribble)
  "unchecked"      -- a checkbox with a genuinely blank interior
  "not_a_checkbox" -- anything else: a table cell, a letter or digit, a line junction,
                      a fragment of text, or empty paper

The region is centred on the candidate; ignore other checkboxes near the edges of the crop.
A checkbox is a small square a person can mark. A cell containing a number, a word, or an
abbreviation is NOT a checkbox, however square it looks.
